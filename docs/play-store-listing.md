## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Slimora** is your all-in-one health companion, built to help Malaysians (and everyone!) eat smarter, move more, and reach their ideal weight — without the overwhelm.

🍽️ **Smart Food Logging**
Log your meals in seconds. Search from thousands of foods including popular Malaysian dishes — Nasi Lemak, Roti Canai, Char Kway Teow, and more. Snap a photo with AI food recognition, scan barcodes, or add food manually. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks all tracked in one place.

🔥 **Personalised Calorie Targets**
Get your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) calculated based on your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. Whether you want to lose weight, maintain, or gain muscle — Slimora sets the right calorie and macro targets for you.

⏱️ **Intermittent Fasting Timer**
Follow 16:8, 18:6, or 20:4 fasting protocols with a beautiful visual clock timer. See your fasting progress in real time and stay on schedule with smart notifications.

💧 **Water Intake Tracker**
Never forget to hydrate. Set your daily water goal and log intake with one tap. Reminders keep you on track throughout the day.

⚖️ **Weight Tracking**
Log your weight daily and watch your progress on a clear chart. Set a target weight and a deadline — Slimora shows you exactly how you're trending.

🏋️ **Workout Tracker**
Log your workouts and see the calories burned factored into your daily budget. Track consistency and build healthy habits over time.

🏆 **Streaks & Badges**
Stay motivated with logging streaks and achievement badges. Celebrate milestones — every day counts.

🌐 **Bilingual — English & Bahasa Melayu**
Switch between English and BM anytime from your profile. Built with Malaysians in mind.

📊 **Macro Tracking**
See your protein, carbs, and fat breakdown for every meal and every day. Hit your macro goals alongside your calorie target.
